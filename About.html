BEHIND THE ANALYSIS

Curious by nature. Analytical by practice.

My journey into data analytics started with curiosity. I wanted to understand how technology works, how information moves, and how numbers can explain what is happening beneath the surface.

I explored different areas of technology before discovering a stronger connection with data analysis. Today, I work with Excel, SQL, Power BI and Tableau to turn raw information into something understandable and useful.

For me, analysis is not simply about producing a dashboard or writing a query. It is about asking better questions and finding the story hidden inside the data.
